# Deeper, with `--full`

Two more points for step 1, answered in the same one or two concrete lines each.

- **Outcome.** What the user wants to happen once the task is done. If aiming at that changes the task, aim at that.
- **Other fields.** The task as verbs (what has to happen, to whom, under what constraints), then two to four fields that would attack it differently, one of them adversarial. For each, what a practitioner of that field would do here, stated as their methods.
